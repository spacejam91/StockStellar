What the score means. score_pct is a 0–100 percentile of criteria strength within that session's eligible universe: 92 means only 8% of the universe fired this hard on these criteria. It is not a probability of gain, not a forecast, and not a recommendation. The only probability-shaped figures here are the measured base rates at /api/scan/base-rates, which report what actually followed each score band, with sample sizes and standard errors.
